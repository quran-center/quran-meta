The code behind this

Eight riwayas

Hafs, Shuba, Warsh, Qalun, Douri, Sousi, Bazzi and Qunbul, each with its own tree-shakeable entry point.

Riwaya conversion

Map an ayah between the numbering of any two riwayas, derived by aligning the KFQC and quranpedia texts.

Every division

Juz, hizb, rub' al-hizb, thumun al-hizb, mushaf pages, rukus, manzils and sajdas, with iterators over each.

Typed to the range

Surah is 1 to 114 and Juz is 1 to 30 at the type level, with type guards and assertion functions.

Your own layout

Swap in a different page list (Indo-Pak, Madinah 1405) with customizeRiwaya.

Surah names in 22 languages

Arabic, English, Urdu, Indonesian, French, Russian, Turkish and more, loadable one language at a time.
